Child Safety Standards

HEG (Hyper Engineering Group)

HEG ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to keeping children safe across all of our applications ("Apps"). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our published standards against CSAE, in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Our Commitment Against CSAE

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) refers to any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children, including grooming, sextortion, child sex trafficking, or otherwise sexually exploiting a child. We strictly prohibit CSAE and CSAM in connection with our Apps and take active steps to prevent, detect, and address it.

2. Prohibited Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited when using our Apps:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, storing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to sexually exploit or abuse a minor.
  • Sextortion or any threat to expose sexual content involving a minor.
  • Facilitating or promoting the sexual exploitation or trafficking of a child.
  • Any other content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

3. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Users can report CSAE, CSAM, or any child safety concern to us directly without leaving the app, or by contacting us using the details below. We review reports promptly and act in accordance with these standards and applicable law.

Child Safety Point of Contact: HEG Child Safety Team

Email: me@heg.wtf

4. How We Address CSAM

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM in connection with our Apps, we take appropriate action, which may include:

  • Removing the offending content.
  • Suspending or terminating the responsible user's access.
  • Preserving relevant information and reporting to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or an equivalent local authority, as required by law.
  • Cooperating with law enforcement investigations.

5. Compliance With Child Safety Laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where our Apps are available. Our practices are informed by industry best practices, including those published by the Tech Coalition.
